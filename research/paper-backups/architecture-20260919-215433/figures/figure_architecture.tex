The concept layer provides stable handles and relation neighborhoods; the version layer records observation-time revisions and conflicts; and the evidence layer resolves each address to an episode, chunk, and exact source span. During \emph{write}, uncertain mentions may remain split and later converge; during \emph{read}, the agent traverses concepts and relations, checks compatible versions, opens source evidence, and stops when the evidence gate is satisfied. Concept--version and version--evidence links make the representation revisable without changing the authority of the original span.}
