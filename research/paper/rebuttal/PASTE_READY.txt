=== BLOCK 2: OFFICIAL COMMENT TO REVIEWER f1J5 ===

We thank the reviewer for flagging PlugMem - it should be cited, and we will add it in the revision (together with AriGraph, G-Memory, GAM raised by another reviewer). We respectfully submit that, once cited, PlugMem strengthens our positioning rather than weakening it.

PlugMem and Deep-Dream optimize opposite objectives on one axis. PlugMem's premise is that "decision-relevant information is concentrated as abstract knowledge rather than raw experience": it distills episodic memory into a propositional + prescriptive knowledge graph and maximizes information density, targeting task-agnostic pluggability and cross-task transfer. Deep-Dream's premise (Sec. 1) is that at write time one cannot know which details will matter for a later decision, so the store must not pre-compress: source spans remain authoritative and immutable, and graph entries are addresses - "search handles, not replacement facts" (Sec. 3.5). Retention of the raw record is what gives Deep-Dream its three formal properties, none of which a distillation pipeline provides: (i) Prop. 1's data-processing inequality - any distilled summary sigma = g(O_t) satisfies I(Z; sigma) <= I(Z; O_t), i.e. distillation cannot raise the recall ceiling, and our design deliberately preserves it; (ii) non-destructive merging - wrong entity-identity decisions remain reversible (candidate families are coarsened, sources never overwritten); (iii) the version projection pi_t - every historical graph state is reconstructable, not just edge-validity windows.

"More comprehensive" depends on what the memory is for. PlugMem is comprehensive for its objective - maximizing decision-relevant density for pluggable transfer. Deep-Dream is comprehensive for a different objective - evidence fidelity under adversarial or self-contradicting histories: every answer cites immutable spans, identity can be re-decided, and past states can be audited. The two are complementary designs at opposite ends of the compression-fidelity axis; neither subsumes the other. The benchmarks agree: PlugMem evaluates on long-horizon dialog QA, multi-hop retrieval, and web agents; Deep-Dream on MAB, LoCoMo, LongMemEval, MEME - no dataset overlap.

On effectiveness comparisons: Table 2's cross-system rows are labeled per answerer/judge and were never claimed as controlled; the controlled evidence is the same-answerer ladder (fixed 33.3 -> tool 40.0 -> source 71.0, full-context 78.7@614k) and the K3-substitution LoCoMo row where Deep-Dream runs under Mem0's own protocol (95.19%, dual judge). We will add PlugMem to the related-work axis table in the revision with the delta above.

We believe the missing-citation issue is fully fixable at camera-ready, and the axis positioning above shows the contribution remains distinct.
