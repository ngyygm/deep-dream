\section*{Ethics Statement}

The study uses publicly available benchmark data and recorded software runs. It does not collect personal data, involve human participants, or make decisions about individuals. The reported examples are used only to evaluate memory retrieval and evidence tracking. We follow the licenses and attribution requirements of the benchmark and software sources.

\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

The released supplementary package contains the run manifests, configurations, prompts, per-item outputs, recomputation scripts, and integrity hashes needed to reproduce the reported tables. Appendix~\ref{app:ledger} describes the artifact ledger, while the remaining appendix sections give the sampling frame, model and judge roles, alignment study, and audit details. Results are regenerated from the frozen artifacts rather than entered manually.
