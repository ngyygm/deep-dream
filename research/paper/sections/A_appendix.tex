\subsection{Artifact ledger}
\label{app:ledger}

The machine-readable ledger is generated by \texttt{paper/results/aggregate\_existing.py}. It records the source summary, dataset, and per-item JSONL SHA-256 values for every run the paper cites, and is regenerated and compared byte-for-byte with the script output before paper writing. The current ledger covers the MemoryAgentBench tracks (two engine generations $\times$ three rungs, official scorer at commit 455306d, identical \texttt{entity2id} fingerprint), LongMemEval full-ingest pairs and the 500-question run, LoCoMo-1986/1540 and the K3-as-answerer diagnostic, LoCoMo-Plus-401, the X7 channel diagnostic, the source-expansion diagnostic, the repaired budget-frontier replay, MEME, BigCodeBench, and ALFWorld. Per-run manifests record dataset and prompt provenance, model identifiers, tool observations, evidence IDs, latency, exit status, and code/runtime hashes; failed integrity checks and negative results are retained rather than dropped. The ledger-generation and recompute scripts ship with the released supplementary material so the chain is regenerable end to end.

\subsection{MemoryAgentBench sampling frame and contamination note}
\label{app:sampling}

The sampled subset was drawn by \texttt{mab\_sample.py} with a fixed scope list recorded in \path{mab-sample.scopes.txt}: 767 questions over 10 scopes and 59 documents against the full 3{,}671 questions over 29 tasks. All four competencies are covered; the omitted tasks are AR's MH-QA, TTL's Recommendation, and 19 tasks whose scopes were not sampled. The same subset and \texttt{entity2id} fingerprint are used for all six ladder runs (three rungs $\times$ two generations). Scope lengths are heterogeneous and disclosed wherever cross-system cells lead: the two FactConsolidation scopes are the 6K variants (26{,}157 characters each); the 32K/64K/262K FC variants (136k--1.1M characters), the 421K RULER scope, and ten other scopes (17 dropped in total) were dropped for budget, all listed with context sizes in the released ledger's subset block; EventQA is sampled at the 64K/128K/full-book scopes rather than only the published full-book average; and the LRU domain carries $n{=}7$ questions ($\infty$Bench-Sum $n{=}1$, DetectiveQA $n{=}6$), so LRU cells are directional. The MAB source corpora are public; whether the answerer's pretraining saw them cannot be excluded or measured post hoc, and every cross-system table row states its own answerer, judge, and subset.

\subsection{Adjusted Overall derivation}
\label{app:adjusted-overall}

The raw Overall (65.1) averages our four domain scores, but two sampled domains omit tasks that score low for everyone. Imputing the omitted tasks at published non-full-context reference values---TTL: our MCC 46 with Recommendation $\approx$13 gives a TTL average $\approx$29.5; AR: our three tasks (92 / 88.3 / 98) with MH-QA $\approx$60 gives an AR average $\approx$84.6; LRU 41.7 and SF 80.0 unchanged---yields an adjusted Overall of $\approx$59.0, and varying the imputed values across the published non-FC range gives 59--61. We report the adjusted estimate beside the raw one because the raw one is biased upward by composition; both are stated beside the best published full-context row (49.6), which differs in answerer, judge, and subset---the comparison is contextual, and no claim in the paper subtracts across calibers.

\subsection{Per-rung prompts and configurations}
\label{app:configs}

All rungs use kimi-k3 as answerer with per-rung prompts archived verbatim in the run manifests and code snapshots listed in \cref{app:ledger}; the released supplementary material reproduces them in full. The $v1{\to}v2$ engine configurations differ as: max output tokens 16{,}384$\to$32{,}768; usable context window 32k$\to$262{,}144; retries 2$\to$5; timeout 300\,s$\to$1{,}200\,s; absolute-path persistence enabled in $v2$. Rung policies: rung~1 one fused retrieval call (top-$k$ spans, neighbor expansion) plus one answer call; rung~2 bounded tool loop over read-only memory tools with a separate answerer over submitted evidence; rung~3 scope materialization then a bounded verify-then-answer loop with shell tools and evidence-gated submission.

\subsection{MAB ingest manifest gaps}
\label{app:manifest-gaps}

The $v1$ and $v2$ MAB ingest runs share a 45-document paired intersection. Manifests missing from one generation are excluded from per-document efficiency ratios, which are therefore computed on the intersection only ($609{\to}329$ calls/doc, $-46\%$); token ratios use the same intersection. Question-level results are unaffected: all 767 questions run against frozen libraries in both generations.

\subsection{Budget frontier}
\label{app:budget}

An earlier depth-10-versus-depth-20 replay is retained as an integrity finding: its prefix invariant failed on all 210 questions (\texttt{primary\_result\_invariant\_violations=210}), so it is not used for any positive claim. The repaired design runs retrieval once per query at maximum depth and slices a fixed ranking to $k\in\{1,3,5,10,20\}$; prefix monotonicity then holds by construction (zero violations), and the frontier rises monotonically from $k{=}1$ to $k{=}20$ with per-$k$ percentile-bootstrap CIs recomputed from the frozen replay artifacts.

\textbf{The K3-substitution row.}
\label{app:k3-substitution}
The single Deep-Dream $J$ row placed inside Mem0's $J$ column is produced by running Mem0's exact binary-judge protocol with Kimi-K3 substituted for GPT-4o-mini as \emph{both} the answerer and the judge---the only change is the model. This yields 95.19\% under the same-model K3 judge (97.38/93.62/80.21/95.33 S/M/O/T) and 94.22\% under an independent Qwen3.7-plus cross-judge on the same K3 answers (96.91/92.91/77.08/93.46; cross-judge agreement 98.51\%). Two differences from Mem0's published $J$ are admitted: it is a single run rather than a 10-run mean$\pm$std, and the model is Kimi-K3 rather than GPT-4o-mini. We additionally disclose an integrity caveat: the run manifest carries \texttt{diagnostic\_override=\{kind:legacy-missing-runtime-code-fingerprint, formal\_claim\_allowed:false\}}, because 1{,}529 of the 1{,}540 answer rows originate from a legacy source run (\texttt{locomo-full-quality-v1}) that predates runtime-code fingerprinting. The answers themselves are unchanged (\texttt{answer\_rows\_unchanged=true}, answer SHA-256 76af5772) and the per-item JSONL carries \texttt{runtime\_code\_sha256} on all 2{,}063 rows (3 distinct hashes: 2fa32774 on 2{,}019, 36b45933 on 33, 42afb1ac on 11), the same dataset SHA-256 (79fa87e9) and library snapshot (cf2e76b8) as the Qwen-answerer run, and the same Mem0 binary-judge prompt (repository \texttt{mem0ai/memory-benchmarks}, commit 4b61c5d, mode \texttt{unified-no-evidence-exact-direct}). We therefore report 95.19\% as a diagnostic cross-judged result, not a fingerprint-complete formal entry.

A stricter controlled comparison---freezing the same answer model, judge model, prompt, temperature, evidence budget, dataset revision, and Graphiti/Zep implementation commit---remains the only path to a shared leaderboard, but it is not a submission hard gate now that the published numbers are cited directly.

\subsection{An information-theoretic reading of the architecture}
\label{app:info-theory}

This appendix gives the paper's two design commitments a formal shape. Every statement is either a structural property of the architecture (checkable against the schema and code) or a conditional proposition with explicit assumptions; none upgrades the causal wording discipline of \cref{sec:ladder,sec:provenance-ablation}.

\textbf{Write time: alignment as deferred decision.} Let the source stream be immutable documents, each extracted mention carry a feature vector (name and content embeddings, attribute assertions, relation-endpoint co-occurrence), and each family hold an append-only observation list linked to exact source spans. Merging is non-destructive---the losing family's observations re-attach with provenance, and merges are idempotent---so alignment errors are revisable coarsenings, not information loss.

\begin{proposition}[Revisability]
\label{prop:revisability}
In the append-only data model, any coarsening of the current family partition is realizable at any later time by non-destructive merges, with no loss of evidence. A store that instead overwrites an entity record with a running summary $\sigma$ satisfies $I(Z;\sigma) \le I(Z;O)$ (data processing inequality), strictly when the summary discards identity-discriminative detail---a loss no later evidence can restore.
\end{proposition}

First-shot alignment accuracy is therefore not the quantity that matters; evidence retention is. The system's conservative thresholds (low-confidence merges degrade to creating a new family) deliberately bias early errors toward over-splitting---the recoverable direction, since a wrong merge contaminates one handle with two entities' evidence and is strictly harder for the read path to detect.

\begin{proposition}[Convergence under pooled evidence]
\label{prop:convergence}
Assume observation features are i.i.d.\ given entity identity, with distinct entities $e \neq e'$ emitting $P_e \neq P_{e'}$ of Chernoff information $C(P_e,P_{e'}) > 0$. Then the error probability of the same/different-entity test on two families with $k$ pooled observations decays as $\exp(-k\,C(P_e,P_{e'}))$ \citep{chernoff1952measure}; in particular, the probability that a true merge---split early under weak evidence---remains undetected decays exponentially in corroborating evidence, independent of the initial segmentation.
\end{proposition}

The assumptions are idealizations (features correlate across windows; same-name-different-entity pairs are exactly low-$C$ cases), and the measured quantities are two engine generations, not a controlled evidence sweep. What the mechanism correspondence supports: window-batch adjudication pools a window's candidates into one decision, and the scope-end sweep re-judges duplicate groups with full-library context to a fixed point---each raises evidence per decision. The measured joint effect is consolidation quality and ingest efficiency improving together: same-name duplicate families fall 340$\to$124 (MAB) and 482$\to$99 (LME) while ingest calls per document fall 46--65\% (\cref{app:ledger}). Relation-endpoint co-occurrence, injected as alignment evidence, is a high-discrimination feature in exactly this sense---shared relational neighborhoods separate the same/different hypotheses---and the same edges later widen the retrieval graph.

\textbf{Store: an index, not a reconstruction.} The derived layer is never answer text: all three rungs' answerers consume verbatim spans (retrieval resolves candidates back to source episodes and snippets). Reconstruction distortion is thus structurally zero---reconstruction never occurs---and all loss is \emph{addressing} loss: the derived layer may lose information about how to find a fact, never about the fact itself. Candidate generation runs over the family index (full-text search, embedding similarity, graph neighborhoods, reciprocal-rank fusion), so macro concept retrieval and span-level resolution form one deterministic chain whose cost scales with index size, not source bytes.

\textbf{Read time: one-sided error, query-chosen distortion.} Because the evidence gate admits only identifiers surfaced by tool calls and cited spans are functions of the immutable sources alone, any overlay perturbation (a stale relation, a wrong scope materialization) enters the answer only through what is read---it can change coverage, never the authenticity or auditability of what is cited. This is the formal content of ``a missed scope, never a fabricated fact'' (provenance reachability, not entailment, \cref{sec:deep-dream}). Likewise, a summarizing memory fixes a rate--distortion point at write time that every future query inherits \citep{shannon1959coding}; a source-grounded system defers that choice to query time---the answerer's reading policy selects its own operating point. The ladder's measured points---(25.1k tokens, 0.324), (35.0k, 0.407), (96.0k, 0.651) Overall, replicated across generations (\cref{sec:ladder})---trace agents choosing progressively lower-distortion points at higher rate, with the hardest verification tasks separating most (FC-MH 2--4 vs.\ 67--70). The framing claims this association, not single-factor causality, and the X7 equivalence bound still caps the derived channels' offline addressing contribution at $\pm$2.4 points: their measured value is selection, not recall (\cref{sec:provenance-ablation}).
