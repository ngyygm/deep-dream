\section*{AI Use Statement}
\label{sec:ai-assistance}

In this work, we used generative AI tools (including OpenAI Codex) to draft, restructure, and edit the manuscript; to prepare a Chinese reading version; to search for related literature and bibliographic information; to assist with analysis and plotting code; and to formulate the information-theoretic interpretation of the architecture, including mathematical claims and their supporting arguments (progressive entity alignment, search--evidence separation, and query-dependent reading budgets), as well as the memory-hierarchy and addressing framing that interprets context as a working set and the memory store as an addressed structure. Generative image tools were used to create and revise explanatory framework figures. We did not use generative AI tools to produce reported results: no synthetic evaluation dataset was created in this workflow, and all reported benchmark scores are drawn from recorded experimental runs rather than generated estimates.

We have reviewed all AI-assisted work. Reported numbers are regenerated from frozen run artifacts by the released recomputation scripts rather than transcribed from AI output; AI-suggested references were checked against the original publications; and the mathematical statements are presented with their assumptions and tied to the measured values rather than taken as unconditional theorems. AI assistance does not constitute independent proof verification or empirical validation.

We take responsibility for the final content of this work, including text, claims, and artifacts produced with the aid of generative AI.
