\section{Analysis and Limitations}
\label{sec:analysis}

\subsection{What the decomposition does and does not show}

The ladder of \cref{sec:ladder} is a systems comparison (\cref{tab:rung-differences}): each rung delta is \emph{consistent with} source verification carrying quality and upper-bounds its marginal effect, never a single-factor estimate---the single-factor evidence is the channel ablation's equivalence bound plus the gate's deterministic enforcement, which locate the overlay's value in navigation, not recall; replication across engine generations is pattern robustness, not causal isolation. The strongest honest statement: the evidence path carries the quality, and the demoted index did not pay for the demotion in recall or in Overall.

\subsection{Positioning, read conservatively}

Three reading rules govern \cref{tab:mab-cross-system}. First, no same-model full-context anchor exists: the kimi-k3 endpoint was retired mid-project and all results are frozen per-item artifacts. Second, our rows are a sampled subset (767 of 3{,}671); the composition-adjusted Overall is 59--61 (\cref{app:adjusted-overall}) against a best published 49.6 beside the raw number. Third, the LME(S$^{*}$) cell (88.3 vs.\ 55.7) is the one judge-involved comparison; the headline cells (SH-QA 92 vs.\ 87, DetectiveQA 83.3 vs.\ 77.5, EventQA 98 vs.\ 82.6, FC-MH 70 vs.\ 7) are substring/fixed-accuracy scored and immune to judge concern, though EventQA and the FC cells carry disclosed length mismatches (\cref{app:sampling}).

\subsection{Limitations}

(1) No same-model anchor; the model-substitution objection is bounded by four published facts (\cref{sec:cross-system}), not closed. (2) Sampled subset (8 of 29 tasks over 10 scopes); omitted low-scoring tasks bias the raw Overall \emph{up}, corrected by the adjusted estimate. (3) LME full-ingest uses $n{=}25/27$ paired samples---labeled, small, ancillary. (4) The sandbox path costs an agentic loop (\RungThreeCost{} vs.\ \RungOneCost{} per question), counted as part of the result. (5) MEME deletion is 0\%; negative-memory semantics need a query-time suppression filter (future work). (6) TTL trails full-context structurally; the lever (atomic-fact extraction density) is untested. (7) LoCoMo $J{=}95.19$ is a single run with a disclosed legacy-fingerprint caveat (\cref{app:k3-substitution}). (8) MAB corpora are public; pretraining contact cannot be excluded post hoc. (9) The LME skill--baseline tie is a property of that paired set.

\subsection{Future work}

The negatives name the next experiments: a query-time suppression filter for deletion and absence semantics; denser atomic-fact extraction for TTL; a trivial-selector control (BM25/embedding top-$m$ scope materialization) to price the overlay's navigation role; the full 3{,}671-question run; and a fingerprint-complete same-model rerun when a stable endpoint exists.
