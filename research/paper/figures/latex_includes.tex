  \caption{Deep-Dream as a document-first temporal concept graph. Processing time is the single state axis: each write appends a source-linked observation/version, while concepts and relations provide a revisable search index. During \emph{write}, uncertain mentions may remain separate and later converge; during \emph{store}, families and relation edges expose low-cost coarse search while episodes and exact spans remain authoritative; during \emph{read}, the agent progressively expands the search, opens source evidence, and stops when the citation check passes. The graph finds candidates; verbatim source spans authorize the answer.}
